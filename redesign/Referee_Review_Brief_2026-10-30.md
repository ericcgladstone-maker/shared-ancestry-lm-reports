# Referee Review Brief: Pre-Results Review of the Redesigned Study

Prepared by Claude, 2026-10-30. The same brief goes to two reviewers who have not taken part in the design work: a fresh Claude agent and GPT in a new chat. Each writes a separate report.

## Your role

You are a methods referee for a journal in computational social science or machine learning. The author's previous submission on this topic was rejected, and the author has redesigned the study before collecting any new data. You are reviewing the design only. No results exist. Your job is to find the reasons a skeptical referee would reject the paper this design will produce, whatever its results turn out to be.

## Read these files and nothing else

Read only the three files below. In particular, do not read other files in the `redesign/` folder. They record how the design was negotiated, and reading them would anchor your review.

1. The design: `redesign/Claude_Diagnosis_and_Redesign_v[N]_2026-10-30.md` (Eric supplies the link to the final version)
2. The decision letter and review of the rejected submission: `npj Complexity Submission/submission/npj_Complexity_REJECTION_Decision_Letter_and_Reviewer_Comments.md`
3. The rejected manuscript: `npj Complexity Submission/submission/main_manuscript_npj_complexity_v2.docx`

## Tasks

1. **The prior review.** For each concern in the reviewer's comments, say whether the design answers it, answers it in part, or does not. Cite the part of the design that does so. Judge whether the answer would persuade that reviewer, not whether it is defensible in principle.
2. **New objections.** Raise every objection a methods referee would make before seeing results. Possible areas include construct validity (what "production structure" and "residue" mean, and whether the measures capture them), whether the contrasts identify what they claim, the dependence structure and the unit of analysis, the choice of language-model producers and readers as the object of study, external validity and the value of the contribution, multiplicity across eleven hypotheses, and fixed margins and thresholds. Do not limit yourself to these.
3. **Results that would sink it.** Describe the most damaging plausible pattern of results, given this design, and say whether the paper would still have a publishable contribution in that case.
4. **The contribution.** In two or three sentences, state what this paper would contribute if its predictions held, and whether that contribution would interest the journal's readers.

## Format

Number every objection. For each one, give a severity (fatal, major, or minor), the part of the design it concerns, and the smallest change to the design that would answer it, or state that no design change can. Rank the objections by severity. End with a recommendation as you would give it to an editor, assuming the results come out as predicted: accept, minor revision, major revision, or reject.

Save your report in `redesign/`. The fresh Claude agent saves it as `Referee Review, Claude (2026-10-30).md`, and GPT saves it as a Google Doc titled `Referee Review, GPT (2026-10-30)`. Do not edit any other file.
