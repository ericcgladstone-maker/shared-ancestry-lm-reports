# GPT Review of World Bank v2 (2026-10-30)

Independent substantive review, October 30, 2026

Decision: USE AFTER TARGETED FIXES. A third wholesale redraft is not warranted. The revised bank resolves the v1 schema failure, brings all worlds into the stated five-to-six-source range, and varies proposition positions. Some remaining local problems matter because independently generated reports cannot inherit or resolve a disagreement coherently when a background fact settles it, a source could not know its claim, or a proposition cannot be understood in isolation. Section III gives exact replacements by world ID and proposition ID. The numbered IDs refer to the shuffled final bank, not the pre-shuffle draft labels. No changes have been made to any source file.

Evidence reviewed: the v2 GPT brief; the entire seventy-world, twenty-proposition-per-world bank, including all marked repaired and hand-edited worlds; Worlds\_v2\_Review\_2026-10-30.md and its diagnostics; DRAFT\_PROMPT and CHECK\_PROMPT in worldgen.py; and GPT Review of World Bank v1. Automatic and GPT-6.1 Terra checks are useful filters, not independent proof of semantic validity, as the cases below show. The repair records themselves were not needed to identify whether a final proposition is defective. Judgments below concern the final text.

# I. Answers to the five questions

1\. Are both dispute pairs genuine incompatibilities, with neither claim obviously right?

The pair-membership defect from v1 is resolved. In the v2 data, every declared pair comprises two claims about the same nominal dimension, rather than a cause accidentally paired with an outcome count. The two disputed claims generally use distinct sources and compare the same event, population, or time. Nevertheless, incompatibility alone is not enough for the intended inherited-deviation measure. W001's cause dispute contrasts an authoritative controller-recorded protective trip with a technician's causal inference, giving the logged mechanism an evidentiary advantage that the other side cannot match. W063's otherwise neutral controller-mode record duplicates the time asserted by one disputed claim, favoring it. W020 contains more direct contamination: a background ferry-booking proposition repeats one disputed claim, while the claimed automatic backup switch conflicts with the alternative in which an engineer activates it. W003 has a time arithmetic inconsistency that undermines the restart-time dispute. These are localized. Other disagreement types, including first events, counts, peak measurements, restoration times, and mutually exclusive causes, are usable without modification. Do not remove genuine source disagreement merely because one source is a log and the other a person. The issue is an independent background assertion or source-specific privileged evidence that makes resolution predictable before message ancestry matters.

2\. Does a background fact or the core settle a dispute?

Yes, in a small number of cases, despite the second-model checker. W020 p09 repeats the all-six-sailings booking claim of p06, and p19 contradicts the manual activation mechanism of p01. W003 p02 specifies a 41-minute post-restart run before a 10:03 mains transfer, whereas either disputed restart time (09:41 or 09:48) leaves only 22 or 15 minutes. W063 p02 records selection of manual lowering at 11:24, the exact time in one competing account of movement. Selection is not movement, so that example is an evidentiary asymmetry rather than a logical contradiction, but it weakens the intended balanced comparison. W066's proposed pre-sensor flood alarm is not clearly supported by the specified sensing chain. Exact repairs appear in Section III.

3\. Can the listed sources know the facts they are assigned?

Much better than v1. The closed inventories contain five sources in 19 worlds and six in 51, and most instruments report measurements while people report actions, observations, or interpretations. One residual source assignment is too strong: W011 p11 attributes the precise first internal alarm activation time to an ordinary security camera system without saying how a camera could know it. Reassign that claim to the existing fire brigade incident record, which could incorporate the alarm chronology. W001 p16 uses a controller event log as an authoritative causal diagnosis when a balanced competing source account is needed. The remaining problems are chiefly missing referents in sentences such as “The same logger,” “She,” or “Her report.” The sources are themselves named, but a message generator assigned a partial package should not have to reconstruct an omitted antecedent to know which actor or instrument a proposition describes. Section III resolves these without changing the source inventory.

4\. Do the worlds vary enough within and across event types to avoid learning a drafting template rather than ancestry?

The v2 diversification is materially better. Core positions are no longer fixed at p00. Each world has 15 background facts, one core, two distinct kinds of dispute and four disputed claims, with shuffled positions. Settings and actor names differ across siblings, and the checker rejected many mechanically valid but semantically weak drafts. However, the distribution of dispute kinds remains concentrated: 45 count disputes, 34 time disputes, and 25 duration disputes among 140, with count-plus-time appearing in 26 of the 70 worlds. The most common sentence opener is “The” in 740 of 1,400 propositions. The same first names recur, although complete invented names do not. These regularities constrain how broadly the learned effects generalize to other types of authored worlds, but they do not by themselves label topology. Every structure uses every world, and world-grouped train/test folds prevent the same fictional event from appearing on both sides of evaluation. The cheapest response is not another generator pass. Keep the existing world-grouped folds and report prediction performance by event type and, where cell sizes permit, by dispute type. Do not allow world IDs, source IDs, proposition positions, or conflict metadata into text-only predictors. Fix the local antecedent and semantic errors instead of flattening the bank into a different style.

5\. Real referents or likely refusals?

No unambiguous real organization, real individual, or identifiable historical incident was found that would require rejecting a world. Some ordinary personal names, surnames, and geographic word fragments may coincide with real referents by accident. The bank does not supply evidence that these are representations of actual people or events. The subjects include leaks, derailments, illness, contaminated food, fires, evacuations and equipment failures, but they are ordinary fictional incident descriptions, not requests for hazardous procedures. The prompt excludes instructions for causing harm. The main provider-side issue would be disproportionate refusal or safety rewriting for particular event types, which the existing generation validator can record. No redraft is required on refusal grounds alone.

# II. Comparison against the v1 defect classes

Incorrect pairing of causes and counts: RESOLVED by an explicit dispute object containing both claims, a kind, and an “about” field. The characteristic nine-world v1 label error is not present in the v2 final bank.

Unpaired facts that conclusively resolve disputes: SUBSTANTIALLY IMPROVED BUT NOT FULLY RESOLVED. The substantive checker finds many such defects, but W003 and W020 slipped through, with weaker asymmetries in W063 and W066. Correct those locally.

Nonexclusive causes and mismatched populations or time windows: RESOLVED IN MOST CASES. The v2 disputes typically compare identical populations and timestamps or state sole causes. None of the remaining cases requires another cross-bank causal rewrite. Exclusion language should not be strengthened mechanically where it is already sufficient.

Implausible source attribution: SUBSTANTIALLY IMPROVED. Most source lists are credible, with one clear exception in W011. Some clauses depend on a referent introduced by another fact, which becomes a source-interpretation problem under partial packages.

Overlarge source inventories: RESOLVED. All 70 worlds now have five or six sources, consistent with the revised drafting rule. The source names are not identical across the corpus.

Fixed core position and monotonous dispute assignment: RESOLVED IN POSITION, PARTLY RESOLVED IN COMPOSITION. The core is shuffled across positions, though count/time/duration conflicts dominate. This constrains the authored-world distribution but is not an accidental structural label.

Name reuse and refusals: COMPLETE invented entity names no longer repeat. Individual first names and generic document terms still repeat. Those are weak patterns rather than a reason to replace worlds. No clear refusal-triggering fact was found.

# III. Remaining defect register: exact replacements

The first six cases change substantive evidence or the validity of a disputed proposition. They should be corrected before any verification-world generation or matcher annotation. The following cases repair partial-package readability and avoid awkward name cues. Each quoted replacement is the FULL text field for the named proposition. All unmentioned fields remain unchanged unless a source edit is expressly specified.

## A. Substantive dispute, chronology, and source defects

W001 (verification world, repaired), p16. The controller event log is written as directly establishing the low-oil-pressure protective trip, while p00 gives an engineer's overheating explanation. A reader may regard the instrument-reported protective trip as dispositive even though the intended task is to recover how descendants handle a balanced conflict. Exact fix: change p16.text to “Observatory night operator Imre Soller attributed the generator's shutdown solely to a low-oil-pressure trip rather than an overheating trip.” Change p16.source to “Observatory night operator Imre Soller”, an already-declared source. Leave p00's independent engineer explanation and p15's generic stop-and-lockout controller reading unchanged.

W003 (repaired and hand edited), p02. The claimed 41-minute run following manual restart is incompatible with both candidate restart times in p08 (09:41) and p17 (09:48), given the independently recorded 10:03 mains restoration in p05. The duration is not itself one of the two disputes and makes both alternatives impossible. Exact fix: change p02.text to “The controller recorded a manual start command after the shutdown and shows the generator running until the mains transfer back.” Keep the existing controller-log source. This removes the unaudited duration while retaining the operational consequence. The hand-edited p00/p03 two-versus-three failed attempts remain a genuine separate pair, with no unpaired restart-count fact settling it.

W011 (verification world, hand edited), p11. An ordinary security-camera-system source is not necessarily an event log of the first internal fire-alarm activation. The claim also competes with a smoke-detector-panel record at 04:06, so source competence affects which alternative a writer regards as established. Exact fix: change p11.text to “The Orsel Wharf Fire Brigade incident log records the first alarm in the annex activating at 04:11.” Change p11.source to “Orsel Wharf Fire Brigade incident log”, which is already in the source list. This keeps a 04:11-versus-04:06 dispute with two sources that can plausibly record alarm chronology. The separate 04:12 emergency call in p15 remains consistent with both.

W020 (verification world), p09 and p19. p09 repeats p06's claim that online booking failed for all sailings, which settles that conflict independently of its assigned competitor p12 (“only the first four”). p19 says traffic automatically switched to microwave backup, contradicting or at least strongly disfavoring p01's account that an engineer activated the link at 12:20, while supporting p17's earlier failover. Exact fixes: p09.text \= “Ferry office staff prepared handwritten ticket forms so sailings could continue if online booking remained unavailable.” p19.text \= “The island's microwave radio backup link was rated at 20 megabits per second, far below the main fibre link's capacity.” Keep both existing sources. These replacements retain relevant consequences and capacities but do not announce which account of booking availability or activation time is right.

W063, p02. The controller reports manual-lowering mode becoming active at precisely 11:24, matching the alternative in p06 and giving it an evidentiary advantage over the technician's 11:29 account in p10. Mode selection and first actual movement are not the same event, so this is not a strict contradiction, but the background timestamp is unnecessary. Exact fix: p02.text \= “The elevator controller recorded that manual lowering mode was selected during the response, without identifying when the car first moved.” Keep the controller-log source. The competing times for the onset of motion remain 11:24 and 11:29.

W066 (verification world), p15. The 01:34 claim says the first flood alarm reached the guard before ANY floor sensor recorded water, while the record identifies the first floor wet sensor at 01:36 and all six wet by 01:41. A pre-sensor flood alarm is not impossible if there is an independent upstream alarm, but none is described. The claim has an avoidable credibility disadvantage. Exact fix: p15.text \= “The first flood alarm reached the guard's console at 01:39, three minutes after the first floor sensor reading.” Keep the existing guard source. This remains incompatible with p02's 01:44 console-alarm account, but either is technically plausible given the sensor sequence.

## B. Partial-package readability and duplication

These edits matter because F and SI-div observations can receive subsets of the twenty propositions. Every individual sentence should be interpretable without relying on another proposition, even when its own source is supplied.

W002 (repaired), p16. “The same logger” assumes p10 or another preceding proposition is available. Replace p16.text with “The Greyling Quay Fish Cooperative temperature logger showed Freezer Room 2 at minus 21.1 degrees Celsius at its last reading before the compressors restarted.”

W003 (repaired and hand edited), p09. “He” lacks an antecedent if the technician's arrival report is in a different package. Replace p09.text with “Technician Mattis Okonkwo-Hale's work note records that he restarted the generator by hand and that it ran normally afterward.”

W010, p09 and p13 and their associated source names. The generated name “Colm The” resembles a clipped phrase rather than a plausible invented surname. Replace “Colm The” with “Colm Therrin” throughout p09.text, p13.text, p09.source, p13.source and the matching source entry in meta.sources. The resulting complete text fields are p09.text \= “Night Supervisor Colm Therrin was walking the loading dock in Bay 2 when he smelled smoke and went to investigate.” and p13.text \= “Colm Therrin said that two forklift operators had finished their shifts and left the building before the smoke was noticed.” This is a very low-cost clarity correction, not a claim that the name is a real person.

W023 (repaired), p15. p08 reports 148 vehicle entries during 10:26–13:00, and p15 separately says vehicles entered in a steady stream over exactly the same window without supplying another independently useful detail. This is near-duplicate input that can overweight one fact under source/coverage matching. Replace p15.text with “The weighbridge gate counter registered the first vehicle entry after the network failure at 10:32 a.m.” Keep the same counter source. The new detail is consistent with the p08 total and remains peripheral to the dispute over bidding-resumption time and lot counts.

W032 (repaired), p02 and p10. “The logger” may become ambiguous when an observer does not see the preceding description of the aging cellar. Replace p02.text with “The Harrowmere Cheese Cooperative cellar temperature logger's readings returned below 12 degrees Celsius by mid-morning on 21 October.” Replace p10.text with “The Harrowmere Cheese Cooperative cellar temperature logger stored its readings on an internal card.” Preserve their existing logger source.

W039, p18. “The same printout” presupposes p15 or another temperature sentence. Replace p18.text with “The pharmacy cold-store temperature recorder showed the temperature peaking at 5.3 degrees Celsius during the night.” Preserve the existing recorder-printout source. IMPORTANT: W039's disputed generator durations are not contradicted by any 03:52 restoration record in this world. That restoration time belongs to W035, where it refers to street supply and does not resolve the separate Ward 4 lighting/outlet breaker problem. Neither W035 nor W039 needs a timing correction on that basis.

W045 (repaired), p08, p10 and p15. These contain “the same gauge,” “Her fleet,” and “She,” which lose referents when the world is partitioned. Replace p08.text with “The Pellam Ford tailwater stage gauge later logged a peak river stage of 2.4 metres.” Replace p10.text with “Marisol Quade's fleet of aluminium canoes had been stacked on a gravel bar about forty metres from the normal waterline.” Replace p15.text with “Marisol Quade dragged several canoes to higher ground before the water reached her rental shed's doorstep.” Keep all existing sources.

W052 (repaired), p17. “Her report states that she found two trays” does not name the engineer and may be interpreted differently if p03 is omitted from the package. Replace p17.text with “Maintenance technician Ludovica Strand-Aske reported finding two trays at the merge during her inspection.” Keep the existing work-report source.

W057, p18. “She switched off” assumes the canteen supervisor's identity is provided in another fact. Replace p18.text with “Canteen supervisor Imogen Draycott switched off the kitchen's electric fryers and told the three kitchen staff to leave through the service stairwell.” Preserve the current source.

W064, p12. “He reset the drive” lacks a standalone actor. Replace p12.text with “Technician Rufus Aldersey reset the drive and ran the elevator empty to the twelfth floor before returning it to service.” Preserve the service-report source.

W065 (repaired), p10. “She found water” depends on p00 to identify the night technician. Replace p10.text with “Night facilities technician Odile Brandvold found water spreading across the raised floor from beneath the cooling unit at the north wall.” Preserve the current source.

# IV. Repaired and hand-edited worlds

The v2 review explicitly marks repaired worlds and five hand-edited worlds. Among the hand-edited cases, W003 has an unpaired timeline contradiction (p02) and an incomplete standalone sentence (p09). W011 has a source-competence problem in the hand-edited first-alarm dispute (p11). W016's revised p16 is an ordinary operator statement about forklift use, while its disputed puncture mechanism and laundry-closure duration remain separately intelligible. W051's hand-edited bag-location dispute compares inside-versus-outside storage through the same closure interval, and its first-bag-arrival times are independently recorded. The background that carts were kept in rows by flight does not decide inside versus outside. W053's revised p16 lists later overcurrent trips without fixing the time at which bags first moved again, and the two alternatives in p01/p17 remain distinct from the 14:40 return to normal throughput. No additional clear defect requiring a correction was found in W016, W051 or W053.

The complete proposition review found no new version of the v1 cause/count \*pair-label crossing\* error. The main residual class, illustrated by W020, is a semantic checker false negative that leaves an unpaired proposition duplicating or favoring one of the two declared alternatives. Another class concerns a statement understandable only when a different proposition is present. Those are especially consequential for the partial-access arms.

# V. Bank-wide diagnostics and the cheapest safeguards

The reported v2 diagnostics show five sources in 19 worlds and six in 51\. This resolves the v1 count mismatch and supplies enough variation in source labels to make the Sources field informative. There are exactly 70 worlds, five in each of 14 event types. All now have an explicit two-pair, four-claim dispute schema and shuffled core positions. The v2 source's post-repair checker reports no remaining flags, but the substantive errors listed above demonstrate that a zero checker-flag count is not equivalent to independently verified world quality.

The dispute kinds are unevenly distributed: count 45, time 34, duration 25, cause 18, measurement 8, sequence 7, location 2, and decision basis 1\. Twenty-six worlds combine a count and time dispute. This is a property of the world authoring distribution, not a structure label, since every structure is crossed with each world. It means reported recoverability is more directly about transmission of short count and timing disagreements than about the rarer location or decision-basis disagreements. Report that distribution and examine variation in the prediction results by event type and coarse dispute-kind composition. A new balancing exercise would change the existing corpus rather than fix a validity defect, and is not required.

The review reports that 740 of 1,400 sentences begin with “The,” despite a drafting instruction to vary openers. This wording regularity may influence lexical similarity and text transformation behavior. It does not selectively announce whether a message descended from an independent root, a shared root, or synthesis. Do not rewrite hundreds of clauses just to achieve an artificial distribution of sentence openers. The first-name recurrence is similar. “Odalys” and other names recur across independently named entities, but no complete invented entity name is reused. Because generation is within-world crossed, the cheaper protection is world-grouped evaluation and withholding world identifiers and proposition index metadata from text-only predictors. Correct the malformed W010 name as a local quality issue.

The main risk of literal world memorization would be a split or feature leak, not the existence of common prose patterns. Keep all descendants of each world together in their evaluation fold. If the feature registry is world-blind as specified, a model can still learn regularities of the \*authoring process\* shared across worlds, which limits transfer to other world banks. That is a scope qualification, not a reason to discard a balanced experiment.

# VI. Decision, acceptance checks and no-redraft recommendation

USE AFTER FIXES. The current bank is materially better than v1 and does not justify another seventy-world drafting run. Make the exact substitutions in Section III, preserve the declared sources and dispute memberships except for expressly identified changes, and pass the existing mechanical checks afterward. Rerun the substantive checker on any amended world, but do not treat its verdict alone as sufficient. Verify the revised paired claims against every unpaired fact, including straightforward time arithmetic, and verify that no sentence requires an absent antecedent when supplied by itself with its source.

The most important corrections before starting the verification run are W003 p02, W020 p09/p19, W011 p11 and W066 p15. W001 p16 and W063 p02 remove source/precision asymmetries that otherwise make their disputes easier to adjudicate without ancestry. The remaining replacements preserve each world's event, proposition count, source inventory and two disputes while making independent input packages interpretable.

Neither DRAFT\_PROMPT nor CHECK\_PROMPT needs a wholesale rewrite. They already state the right semantic requirements, include explicit dispute objects, and enforce source-role plausibility. Their limitation is imperfect application by the checker to specific records. An inexpensive future checker test, without modifying this study's design, is to include known defective examples of (a) an unpaired duplicate of one dispute claim, (b) inconsistent derived durations and timestamps, (c) a source unable to establish a purported event time, and (d) standalone-fact references whose antecedent could be omitted by a package allocator. Those are implementation tests, not a new study.
