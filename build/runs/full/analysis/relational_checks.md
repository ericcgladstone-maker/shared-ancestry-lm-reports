## Rarity fit

Rare word-sequence overlap counts the 3- to 5-word sequences two messages share that appear in at most two documents of the fold's training texts. Sets in a block reuse identical messages across structures (the store keeps one call per key), so 900 distinct texts fill the 1,080 message slots of the IND and DEP sets and the 1,620 of the three-structure sets. A training row's shared sequences are then counted in its own and its sibling rows and seldom stay rare, while a test row's texts are absent from the table. Under the fit used so far (fold), the feature is near zero in training rows and large in test rows, and in the three-structure rows it is zero in every training row, so the pair and set models there cannot use it. No test-world information enters either fit. The second fit (own world excluded) removes each training row's world from the counts, so training and test rows see the same kind of table. Every result below is reported under both fits.
