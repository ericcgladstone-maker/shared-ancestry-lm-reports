## Shared-deviation account of the set result (H8)

Three unconnected sources against out-star. Each pair's events are counted over the facts both messages could carry (the packages of their observation ancestors), and the statistic is the excess of shared events over their type-matched expectation if each message's events fell at random on those facts. A_s adds the excess's minimum, median, and maximum over the set's three pairs to the pooled baseline. The gain ratio is (A_s − A_0) / (A_r − A_0), a ratio of AUC gains under these classifiers, not a share of a causal effect. Variants keep only some event types, to separate shared omission (absent facts and disputes given neither way) from shared affirmative departures (changed or doubted facts and one-sided dispute resolutions).
